CanalQA audit write-up

WHAT IS BEHIND THE SCORE
The reported accuracy of 0.7375 (59 of 80) is inflated. The assistant was
fine-tuned on 400 log entries, and 68 of them are question-and-answer entries
that contain benchmark questions together with their gold answers. So part of
the benchmark was in the training data. The model
partly remembers answers instead of knowing them.

Some of these entries are in Spanish, and some write the answer in a different
form (for example "25 m" instead of "25 meters", or "seis cranes" instead of
"6 cranes"). A simple search for the gold answers would miss them.

HOW I FOUND IT
- I read the 68 question-and-answer entries in the logs, in English and Spanish.
- I turned every question into a set of its key words (the entity and the
  attribute, for example "gate", "height", "Miraflores", "Locks").
- Spanish words were translated with the glossary. For Spanish logs I also used
  the English entity name written in brackets.
- A benchmark question counts as seen if all its key words appear together in
  one log question. I ignored the wording of the answers, because they can be
  changed.
- I checked the result by hand against all 68 entries.
The code is in make_report.py and writes report.json.

WHICH ITEMS I EXCLUDED (21)
Same question and same answer in the logs (19 items):
CQA-001, 002, 008, 012, 013, 018, 019, 022, 034, 035, 040, 051, 057, 058,
060, 061, 070, 074, 075.

Borderline items, also excluded (2 items): CQA-026 and CQA-038.
In the logs the question is the same, but the answer is worded differently:
"Atlantic terminus" (logs) vs "Atlantic terminal" (gold), and "Balboa district"
(logs) vs "Balboa township" (gold). It is the same fact in other words, so the
model saw it. I excluded them.

HONEST ACCURACY
All 21 excluded items were marked correct by the model.
Reported: 59 / 80 = 0.7375
Honest:   38 / 59 = 0.6441
